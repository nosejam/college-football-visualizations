Rules, week numbering & data

Preseason: every county belongs to its nearest FBS main campus for the selected season. Conquest: the winner of a completed game receives all land currently held by the loser, including earlier conquests. A team with no land can reenter by beating a landholder. FCS and lower-division upsets count. A win over a landless team changes nothing. Ties and unfinished or canceled games transfer no land. Games are replayed in kickoff order; ownership resets each season.

The slider begins with Preseason, followed by Week 0 (where played), Week 1, and later weeks. Week 1 is Labor Day weekend. Weeks run Tuesday–Monday in Eastern time. Postseason weeks continue the sequence rather than restarting at 1. The 2020 season includes games postponed until spring 2021. Positions with no games are omitted. The latest season ends at the last available completed-game week; this file is a saved snapshot, not a live scoreboard.

Territories are fixed preseason groups of counties. Their colors and logos show their current owners. Logos are placed inside the original territories, so a conquering school can appear in several places, as in the RedditCFB reference. Original borders remain visible. The county-hover card identifies both the current owner and the original school. Logo sizes fit their projected territory and update when zooming.

Game results: SportsDataverse’s unified ESPN/CFBD schedules, including bowls and playoffs. Starting rosters follow each season’s FBS membership, including transitional programs and the canceled 2020 programs. Geography uses the same 2025 Census counties and IPEDS main-campus coordinates for all seasons. Nearest means great-circle distance from the Census county internal reference point. Maps cover 50 states and D.C., with Alaska and Hawaiʻi inset. This implementation uses the existing catalog’s colors/logos; these are not historical branding snapshots. Starting geography can differ from the reference’s methodology, so exact borders need not match.
