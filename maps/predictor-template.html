Predictive power, objective selection, and limitations

SP+ predicts game strength and stays fixed within every simulation, including forced wins and losses. Missing SP+ matchups use the identified Elo baseline; missing ratings are never replaced with average SP+. Elo comparison updates Elo after games. Switching methods preserves your picks. The latest SP+ snapshot cannot be used for earlier weeks; those weeks fall back to Elo.

Game probability uses SP+ point differences plus home field (zero at neutral sites). The normal and logistic mappings were compared chronologically using saved pregame Elo: train 2016–2022, select on 2023, test on 2024–2025. Applying this calibration to SP+ is an unvalidated transfer assumption, not an SP+ backtest. Historical pregame SP+ snapshots are unavailable; snapshots are now archived for prospective validation. No arbitrary conference bonuses or probability clipping.

Strength of Record = −ln P(reference team wins at least your win total against your schedule). A log-space Poisson-binomial calculation accounts for each opponent and location, assuming independent games conditional on fixed ratings. The fixed contender reference is SP+ +20, or Elo 1625 for opponents missing SP+. SOS is one minus that reference's average win probability (higher means harder). Reference strengths are modeling choices; mixed-source opponents and lower-division Elo carry additional uncertainty.

National selection ranks by SOR. Tail probabilities within 1% of the best remaining team form an anchored near-tie group; break ties by SOS, within-group head-to-head win differential, power, then stable team ID. The 1% tolerance is configurable policy, not a fitted weight. Observed objective rank includes completed games and your fixed outcomes; projected rank and record average complete simulated seasons through Selection Day. Quality wins use the independent top 25 power list.

The ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC champions qualify, as does the highest-ranked eligible Group of Six team even without a championship. Notre Dame qualifies when ranked top 12. Fill remaining places in objective rank order. Selected teams retain relative rank order, putting automatic qualifiers outside the top 12 at the bottom. Seeds 1–4 get byes; the bracket never avoids rematches. 2026 CFP rules.

Conference titles use conference records and the existing partial tiebreaker sequence (including Sun Belt divisions and ACC schedule equivalence). Every tied qualification is flagged because complete official league rules are not implemented. Stable IDs resolve residual ties deterministically; there is no committee or tie lottery. Unannounced schedule games cannot be modeled. All source-classified FBS teams are assumed eligible; eligibility bans require a rules update.

“Win out” sets known regular-season games, including both sides of each matchup. Conference-title results may be fixed below when the matchup is scheduled; a pick applies only when those participants qualify. Army–Navy after Selection Day cannot change qualification. Playoff games are then simulated. Common random draws keep unchanged games comparable between scenarios.

~0% and ~100% mean no exceptions observed, not mathematical proof. SOR qualification is checked exactly when at most ten regular/title results remain uncertain; otherwise endpoint estimates remain unproven; conference elimination uses exact optimistic standings feasibility. No Elo ranking bound is reused for SOR. Sampling uncertainty is shown in each probability tooltip.
