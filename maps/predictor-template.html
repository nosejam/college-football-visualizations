How projections and elimination work

Games are drawn using Elo win probabilities; Elo is updated after every drawn or fixed result. Win/Lose next refers to a team's next remaining regular-season game. Win/Lose out fixes every remaining known regular-season game. A fixed win is the opponent's fixed loss. If picks conflict, the most recent edit wins; the affected checkboxes and game list update together. A regular-season game after Selection Day (such as Army–Navy) changes subsequent game strength but cannot change the already selected field. Conference-title games and the CFP are simulated separately, so “win out” does not force a playoff championship.

Conference qualification uses conference records, Sun Belt divisions, the ACC's alternate-schedule rule, head-to-head results, common opponents, opponent conference winning percentage, and a lottery for unresolved ties. This is an approximation of the leagues' complete tiebreakers. Unscheduled matchups (including Pac-12 flex games) cannot be fixed in advance and are not invented.

The four Power Four champions and the highest-Elo Group of Six team qualify automatically. Notre Dame qualifies if top 12 in the simulated ranking. Remaining places are filled by final simulated Elo, then the fixed 12-team bracket is played, with first-round byes for seeds 1–4. Official CFP format.

A displayed 0.0% is reserved for proven elimination under the model's optimistic conference-standings feasibility or Elo ranking bounds. A zero simulation count without a proof is shown as ~0% (not observed), not “eliminated.” Independent teams have no conference title to win. Actual committee judgment and complete league tiebreakers can differ from this model. At-large mathematical elimination cannot be inferred from a loss count alone.

The Elo column shows the selected week's rating, adjusted chronologically by fixed future results only. Unfixed outcomes vary across simulations. Records and observed games use only information available through the chosen week. This is a forecast of the current season, not a backtest of actual CFP selection.
