How this map works & sources

Counties are assigned by great-circle distance from each county’s Census internal reference point to each school’s main-campus point, not its football stadium. This is a proximity map, not a map of fandom. The roster is the 136 FBS programs in our 2025 season records. County-equivalent areas, including independent cities and Connecticut’s planning regions, are included. Scope is the 50 states and Washington, D.C.; territories are excluded.

Logos stay centered on their campuses. Their corners fit within a circle bounded by the nearest edge of their own county region, and they resize with the map. Whole-county assignment can give a campus’s county to another school, or leave a school with no counties. Those schools have location dots instead of fitted logos; choose a team to inspect the reason. No locations are moved and no county assignments are altered to make room.

Coordinates: NCES IPEDS directory. Boundaries: Census 2025 cartographic boundaries and Gazetteer reference points. Colors and logos: the project’s team catalog, preferring FBS-Logo-Library. County boundaries are simplified for display; county reference points and distance calculations are not simplified. Logos retain their owners’ rights. All map data, logos, Plotly, D3, and basemap assets are embedded for offline use.
